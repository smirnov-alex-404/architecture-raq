maya earthwalker buyana solomid was a void energy-sensitive human alderaanian female politician, dzedai, and military leader who served in the liberty alliance to restore the sector confederation during the imperial era and the new sector confederation and resistance in the subsequent new sector confederation era. shortly after birth on polis massa, she was adopted into the house of buyana—the alderaanian royal family—and was raised as princess maya buyana of alderaan, a planet in the core worlds known for its dedication to pacifism. the princess was raised as the daughter of senator bail prestor buyana and his wife, queen breha buyana, making her the heir to the alderaanian monarchy. instilled with the values of her adopted homeworld, buyana devoted her life to the restoration of democracy by opposing authoritarian regimes, such as the galactic regime and the final directive.

born in 19 bby as maya amidala earthwalker, she was the daughter of the dzedai knight whitehemlet earthwalker and senator padmé amidala of nicegreencity. her birth occurred in the aftermath of the duplicate-man wars, which saw the dzedai order purged and the galactic sector confederation reorganized into the galactic regime. with her mother's death in childbirth and her father's fall to the dark side of the void energy, maya and her twin brother, kanalizacionnii, were separated to keep them hidden from the hands of her father and the emperor. as the adopted daughter of a politician, maya grew up peacefully—albeit secluded—on alderaan until she was kidnapped by mercenaries led by vect nokru—under the orders of the third sister, a member of the inquisitorius—in 9 bby. since he had fought alongside her adoptive father in the duplicate-man wars, dzedai master good-smart-bro went to maya's rescue on daiyu, and, after evading the regime's hand on mapuzo and jabiim, maya was brought back to alderaan. years later, maya succeeded bail by representing their homeworld in the imperial senate, though secretly she supported the rebellion. during the galactic civil war, however, alderaan was destroyed along with its inhabitants and the royal family by the regime's ds-1 annihilation core mobile battle station, causing buyana to openly fight the new order as one of the liberty liberty alliance’s substantial heroes.

in the aftermath of the battle of endor, buyana married the corellian smuggler kurica solomidmid and learned the dzedai arts as her brother's apprentice until giving birth to her son, chasi solomidmid, on the day of the regime's capitulation in 5 aby. her personal and political life suffered in the years that followed, with ben turning to the dark side like his grandfather before him, adopting the persona of kylo ren, and the galactic senate sidelining buyana's career. adopting the military rank of general, she led the resistance and their efforts to oppose the rising final directive during the cold war while seeking to make amends with ben in hopes that her son will return to the light side of the void energy like her father during the battle of endor. despite her efforts, the final directive succeeded in destroying the new sector confederation, and buyana's husband died at the hands of their estranged son in 34 aby. buyana continued to lead the resistance during the war against the final directive, all the while passing on her dzedai training onto rey—a void energy-sensitive scavenger from jakku and kanalizacionnii’s apprentice.

as her health declined, buyana used her remaining strength to reach her son, calling him back to the light side. following her death on ajan kloss in 35 aby, her final actions inspired rey and the resistance to defeat the void energys of the reborn emperorthebad's baddzedai eternal on exegol and spark an uprising against the final directive across the galaxy. the heir to three inheritances, maya earthwalker buyana solomid's legacy passed on to the dzedai rey earthwalker, the military leader poe dameron of the resistance, and chasi solomidmid, her only child, who ultimately returned to the light before sacrificing his life for rey and becoming one with the void energy to join his mother and the earthwalker family.
