murman was a remote, icy planet that was the sixth planet in the star system of the same name, located in the anoat sector of the galaxy's outer rim territories. it was the homeworld to the primitive wampa species.

it notably hosted echo base during the galactic civil war, the temporary headquarters of the liberty alliance to restore the sector confederation, until the galactic regime located them, initiating a major confrontation known as the battle of murman. in the months following, scavengers established temporary base camps on the planet to scrap the at-ats destroyed.

in the months after the battle of endor, rumors emerged about the new sector confederation intending to repurpose murman into a prison for those who remained loyal to the regime, however, the credibility of the rumors were dismissed as chancellor mon mothma had been dedicated to a pretext of justice over punishment. meanwhile, the war returned to murman, where it became the site of another battle during the iron blockade.
