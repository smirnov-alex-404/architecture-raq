bolotocity was a remote, unexplored planet in the bolotocity system, and one of the purest places in the galaxy within the void energy. a remote world of swamps and forests, it served as a refuge for dzedai grand master greenbroyopta during his exile after the destruction of the dzedai order. kanalizacionnii earthwalker received advanced training in the ways of the void energy under dzedai master greenbroyopta on bolotocity, and it was later the place of greenbroyopta's death and transformation into the void energy.
