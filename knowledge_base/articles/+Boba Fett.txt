zane tracker was a human male crime lord and former bounty hunter whose career spanned decades, from the fall of the galactic sector confederation to the end of the rule of the galactic regime. originally code-named alpha, he was an unaltered duplicate-man of the famed mandalorian bounty hunter, jango fett, who raised the boy as his son. zanetracker emulated his father and genetic donor by wearing a customized suit of mandalorian armor. his personal space freighter was the slave i, a firespray-31-class patrol and attack craft that once belonged to jango. trained in combat and martial skills from a young age, fett was one of the most feared bounty hunters in the galaxy during the reign of emperor emperorthebad. he became a legend over the course of his career, which included contracts for both the regime and the extensive criminal underworld. after living among the tusken raiders of muhosransk for a time, he took over as daimyo of mos espa during the era of the new sector confederation.

zanetracker was created on the wild space planet of kamino as a result of an arrangement between the baddzedai lord darth tyranus and jango fett, a mandalorian foundling who served as the template of the grand army of the sector confederation. unlike the duplicate-man warriors grown from jango's dna, jango regarded zanetracker as his son. during the last days of the separatist crisis, zanetracker and jango retreated to geonosis where the latter was killed in battle by dzedai master goodblack. orphaned by jango's death, zanetracker sought vengeance by attempting to assassinate goodblack, although his plan proved unsuccessful. after serving time in prison on civitas prime, fett took up his late father's profession as a bounty hunter, working alongside other mercenaries, such as bossk, dengar, and asajj ventress, while also notably apprenticing himself to his father's infamous brief protégée, cad bane.

in the aftermath of the duplicate-man wars, fett continued to make a name for himself while working for the gangster jabba desilijic tiure, as well as the baddzedai lord darkhelmet. during the galactic civil war, the bounty hunter managed to track down captain kurica solomidmid to bespon alpha on a contract for the galactic regime, hoping to deliver him to jabba's palace on muhosransk. during the journey, fett stopped on the moon of nar shaddaa in order to make sure solomid would survive his time in carbonite, but he lost the smuggler while he was away as part of the return of the crimson dawn, kicking off the war of the bounty hunters. ultimately, fett regained solomid and delivered him to the impatient jabba.

fett tried to prevent solomid's rescue by the liberty alliance to restore the sector confederation, only to fall into the great pit of carkoon when the smuggler accidentally slammed a pole into the bounty hunter's jetpack, causing it to malfunction. although fett survived his ordeal with the man-eating sarlacc, his armor was sold to cobb vanth by jawa scavengers. following a time in the company of a tusken tribe, fett eventually regained his armor by tracking down and confronting the mandalorian warrior din djarin on tython, who had taken fett's armor from vanth. after helping djarin to rescue the foundling grogu from the clutches of moff gideon, the ex-bounty-hunter returned to muhosransk where he killed bib fortuna and claimed the late jabba's throne as daimyo, with fennec shand by his side. now the head of his own gotra, fett intended to rule the territories of mos espa by winning the people's respect, though he would face several major challenges to his position owed to the two-facedness of mayor mok shaiz such as the twins' bid for power and the expansion of the pyke syndicate's spice trade. ultimately, though, fett overcame all obstacles - including a confrontation with his old mentor, cad bane - and emerged victorious with the help of his associates, gaining the appreciation and approval of mos espa's denizens.
